Support and FAQ

HG.Art is an augmented reality (AR) application designed specifically for interacting with exclusive artworks by Helena Grinshpun. Simply point your device's camera at the artwork or approach it to the artwork approximately on a distance of 15 cm to activate NFC, and watch it come alive through, approve to proceed with the AR app technology. After a single use on the artwork on your smartphone will be a cached copy (mirror) of the app, that will disappear after 30 days or after the app updates.
The app is compatible with iOS devices running iOS [minimum supported version] or later. Please ensure your device software is up-to-date for optimal performance.

Ensure your device camera has permissions enabled (Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera).

Check your internet connection; AR features may require stable connectivity.

Ensure good lighting conditions and focus your camera on the artwork.

Close and restart the app.

Ensure your app is updated to the latest version via the App Store.

Restart your device to refresh its memory.

We appreciate your feedback! Please send bug reports or suggestions to support@helenagrinshpun.art . Include details about your device and operating system version and a clear description or screenshot of the issue.
You can reach our dedicated support team via email at support@helenagrinshpun.art . We aim to respond within 24-48 hours.
