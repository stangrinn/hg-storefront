MY STORY

Between the canvas and the unseen

The first time I remember feeling true curiosity and creative excitement was at the age of five. My relatives had sent a package of gifts, clothes, and toys from England. Among them was a glossy postcard with a large white bunny on the cover. It immediately caught my eye and held my full attention.

I remember pinning it to the wall and trying to draw the bunny on a piece of paper. The feeling was pure joy — I felt free and fully immersed in the process. That moment stayed with me, planting the seed of what it means to create, even in the simplest way.

In school, I realized how deeply art attracted my attention. As I grew older, I became fascinated by people — their thoughts, behaviors, and emotions — which led me to study psychology. I earned my bachelor’s degree and, in the process of understanding human behavior, I also began to understand myself more deeply. That was when I knew my path was in design.

For over a decade, I’ve worked as a digital product and interface designer, delivering high-performing results across various platforms. And throughout my professional journey, I always made space for my passion — exploring art. I spent my personal time creating collages, digital drawings, customized clothing, and surreal abstract posters.

I went through a long and personal journey searching for my style — exploring the emotions, motifs, and messages behind my work. With the help of modern tools like AI and AR, I finally found the means to bring my visions to life. Today, I am fully immersed in the creative process, both as an artist and a storyteller.

Every artwork you see is a piece of my soul — shaped by my life, emotions, and personal journey.
